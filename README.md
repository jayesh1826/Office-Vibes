# 🏢 Office Vibes - Interactive Ambient Workspace

An interactive, lofi office workspace web app featuring dynamic YouTube music playback, digital LED shift countdown, contextual corporate reactions, mini-games, custom playlist management, and ambient background slideshows.

## ✨ Features
* ⏰ **8-Hour Shift Countdown & Check-In/Out Logger**
* 💬 **Contextual Office Reactions & Fading Corporate Dialogue**
* 💻 **Interactive Laptop Video Frame**
* 📁 **Pomodoro Focus Dossier Timer**
* 📌 **Pinned Sticky Note Goal Tracker**
* 🎮 **Break-time Mini-Games (Tic-Tac-Toe & Coin Flip)**
* 📎 **Custom YouTube / YT Music Playlist Manager**
* 💡 **Interactive Desk Lamp with Flashlight Effect**
